# Known limits

- Android does not permit ordinary third-party apps to silently toggle several protected system settings.
- Call answer/end availability varies by Android version, role, carrier and permission.
- Wake phrase detection is not enabled by this build.
- Voice recognition offline support depends on the installed Android speech engine.
- WhatsApp message sending is not automated in this build.
- Emergency SMS opens the SMS composer for the user to send.
- OEM autostart and battery optimization settings differ by manufacturer.
