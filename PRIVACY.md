# Privacy

Ask AI stores preferences locally on the device. It does not include analytics, advertising SDKs, account login, or embedded AI API keys. If an operator configures a Cloudflare Worker URL, command text sent to that URL is processed by the Worker and its selected AI provider. Avoid sending secrets, OTPs, passwords, PINs, Aadhaar numbers, or payment credentials. Android speech recognition availability and offline behavior depend on the installed speech provider.
